How the fly feels

A model of the drives and feelings a fruit fly can have. They rise and fall with what happens to it: pokes hurt, food pleases, danger frightens, flying tires it out.

Tap a glowing part of the brain

Every glowing shape inside the head is a real brain region or neuron from Virtual Fly Brain, mapped by scientists.

Tap one to read what it does. Use Explore to find more.

Show the fly a video

Paste a YouTube link. It plays on a screen in front of the fly, and the fly reacts to what it sees.

      Meet the fly

      This is a fruit fly with its real brain map inside its head, built from scientists' data at Virtual Fly Brain.

      • Tap a glowing part of the brain to learn what it does.
      • Walk it around with the joystick or W A S D, make it fly, poke it.
      • Show it a YouTube video on the screen in front of it and watch how it reacts.
      • Follow its feelings and the live story of what it is doing.
      Fly's eye view
