Internal states

Not guesses: each gauge is the level of a state variable in the circuit model, updated only by sensory input and time. Each has an identified circuit in fruit-fly neuroscience (tap a gauge for the reference).

Fruit flies do not have human emotions. These are the measurable internal states that change their behaviour, named as the literature names them.

How this works

Sensory input → identified neuron populations → descending neurons → body. Nothing is inferred from text or titles. The model is rate-based and simplified, but every link below is a published pathway. Live values update as you watch.

Internal states and their circuits

Limits

This is a mechanistic sketch, not the 140,000-neuron connectome simulation. Populations are lumped, rates are normalised, and constants are tuned to reproduce the published behaviours (optomotor turning, looming escape, fixation, PER, sleep homeostasis). Colour vision, olfaction, courtship and aggression circuits are not modelled yet.

      Meet the fly

      A fruit fly with its real brain map inside its head (Virtual Fly Brain). Everything it does is computed by a circuit model of named neurons: what its eyes see, what it touches and tastes, and its own internal states.

      • Show it something: share your screen, use your camera, or run a lab stimulus. Its neurons respond live.
      • Poke it, feed it: watch nociceptors, sugar neurons and dopamine react.
      • Tap a glowing brain part to learn what it is and which population it belongs to.
      • Follow its internal states and the live neural monitor.
      Fly's eye view
      Each cell is one sampled ommatidium: the luminance its photoreceptors receive right now. Arrows are the T4/T5 motion signals. Red flash = giant fiber.
